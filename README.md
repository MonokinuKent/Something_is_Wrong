# Something_is_Wrong

## Project Description
**Something is Wrong** is a unique game for people who constantly feel **abandoned** by those **closest to them** or by their **friends**. It is about a person who feels the same thing, abandoned by their friends. So, you have to find out what is wrong with the _main character_ or _their friends_.

## Inspiration
This game is inspired by my life. I didn't know either my friend or I did something wrong until there was an issue between I and my friend. That issue was not resolved between my friend and me, and it will not be resolved by the end of the day. Should I start first? Should I wait for them to resolve the issue together? I didn't know which one should I choose. Regarding that matter, there are only two options: I take the initiative, or I wait for him to start first. However, it is not easy to choose one of those options. I have to identify who is causing the trouble, me or my friend.
